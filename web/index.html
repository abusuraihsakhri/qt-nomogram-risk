The 500-ms threshold is a QTc screening marker, not the Chan nomogram risk line. Formulas can be inaccurate at unusually low or high heart rates.

Method and limitations

Bazett QTc = QT / √RR(s); Fridericia QTc = QT / ∛RR(s). These correct for heart rate but do not estimate an individual probability of arrhythmia.

The published Chan/Isbister nomogram compares the uncorrected QT–heart-rate pair with its own at-risk boundary. Do not substitute the QTc flag for that assessment.

Interpret results alongside manual ECG measurement, QRS width, medications, electrolytes, symptoms and clinical setting. This standalone page does not transmit inputs to a server; do not enter identifiers.

References: Chan et al., 2007; Isbister & Page, 2013.
